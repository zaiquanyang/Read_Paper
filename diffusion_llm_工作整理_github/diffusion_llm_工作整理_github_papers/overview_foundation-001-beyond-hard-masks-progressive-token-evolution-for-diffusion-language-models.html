价值、局限与启发

Adaptation from autoregressive (AR) pretrained models faces training difficulties due to fundamental mismatch between unidirectional causal priors and bidirectional iterative refinement. The inherent discrepancy leads to increased training difficulty and slower convergence for AR-based backbones. Substantial computational resources would be required to override the deep-seated unidirectional bias.
